# Add-on Ideas

Extras that would make the course more useful. They're ranked by how much they help a beginner. Each one can be handed to Claude Code with the "Build an add-on" prompt in [[Claude Code Handoff]].

| # | Add-on | Why it's worth it | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Position-size calculator page** on the site (forex, gold, stocks, crypto: account, risk %, stop → lots/shares, margin) | The #1 skill that keeps beginners alive; turns 0.3 and 3.2 into a tool you use every day | Small |
| 2 | **Personal-finance foundation lesson** (before investing: emergency fund, high-interest debt, what money you can afford to risk) | Investing education without this is incomplete and dangerous | Small |
| 3 | **Historical case studies** module: 1987 crash, 2008 crisis, 2015 Swiss-franc shock (forex stops failing), 2020 COVID crash, 2022 rate hikes, FTX collapse, 2023 SVB, gold's 2011 peak and 2024–25 run | Shows every lesson in a real event; very memorable | Medium |
| 4 | **Trading journal + backtest log templates** in Obsidian, plus a Bases (or Dataview) dashboard: win rate, R, expectancy by setup | Makes Phases 3, 4 and 9 practical | Small |
| 5 | **Flashcards** generated from the Glossary and all `[!check]` questions (Obsidian Spaced Repetition plugin format) | Long-term memory for 300+ terms | Small |
| 6 | **Thailand / Laos specifics**: how to access SET, LSX and US stocks locally, taxes on foreign income and capital gains, Thai tax-saving funds — researched and date-stamped | Makes Phase 10 actionable for you | Medium (needs research) |
| 7 | **Glossary hover tooltips + site search** | Readers can check any term without leaving the page | Medium |
| 8 | **"Demo → micro → live" progression plan** with pass/fail gates (expands 11.1) | Stops people going live too early | Small |
| 9 | **Weekly market review template** (regime, big-three news, levels, GEX context) | Builds the professional habit from 0.8 and 10.x | Small |
| 10 | **Risk disclaimer + "how to use this course" page** on the site | Needed before sharing the site publicly | Small |
| 11 | **Publish the site** (GitHub Pages or Netlify, free) | Lets you study on your phone anywhere | Small |
| 12 | **Printable PDF per phase** | Offline study and review | Medium |
